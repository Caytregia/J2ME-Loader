Bitmap font styles (one folder each: han16, han12).

Shared:
- Latin/Vietnamese glyphs: originally from PhoneME-Turbo 1.2.0 (corebridge-atlas.png),
  plus extra Latin punctuation/symbols drawn by hand.

han16 (16 px hanzi):
- CJK Unified Ideographs (U+4E00-9FFF), Extension A (U+3400-4DBF), compatibility ideographs,
  kana, CJK punctuation, full-width forms and symbols rendered from GNU Unifont
  (http://unifoundry.com/unifont/, GPL-2+ with the font embedding exception / OFL-1.1).

han12 (12 px hanzi):
- Ideographs and punctuation rendered at 12 px from WenQuanYi Zen Hei
  (GPL-2 with the font embedding exception), a few rare characters from Noto Sans CJK (OFL-1.1),
  symbols/arrows and any remaining characters downscaled from GNU Unifont.

game10 (Latin/Vietnamese 10 px, hanzi 11 px):
- Latin/Vietnamese glyphs decoded from the game's own font.bin (provided by the user).
- Extra Latin punctuation/symbols (= _ ` (c) (R) TM quotes, dashes, etc.) taken from the han12 style.
- Ideographs, kana, CJK punctuation and full-width forms rendered at 11 px from WenQuanYi Zen Hei
  (GPL-2 with the font embedding exception); a few rare characters from Noto Sans CJK (OFL-1.1);
  symbols/arrows downscaled from GNU Unifont.
